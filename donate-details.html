Education

Let’s education for children get good life

This program provides education services to children who have no access to school. The targeted children are those from poor families, orphans and street children in Moshi, Kilimanjaro. We support both enrolled students and children who are not yet in school by taking them to class — uniforms, fees and a trusted adult who stays in touch.

Summary

Child sponsorship is one of the most effective ways to help children escape poverty. As you exchange letters, send photos and offer encouragement, your care brings hope that can last a lifetime.

  • Uniforms and school fees so a child can stay in class

  • Each child has only one sponsor, from age 3 to 18

  • The child writes at least twice a year

More than 22 children are currently sponsored in Habari Foundation’s Child Sponsorship Program. We always do a physical assessment of families and schools when selecting children — living standards and real need on the ground.
