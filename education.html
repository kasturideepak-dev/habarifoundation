Education Program

Help a child get into school—and stay there.

Education opens doors to confidence, independence, and opportunity. Habari Foundation works alongside families and schools in Tanzania to help children overcome the barriers that interrupt their learning.

Through sponsorship, school essentials, and ongoing support, we help children facing poverty and difficult circumstances continue their education.

Why Education Matters

The opportunity to learn should belong to every child.

For families experiencing financial hardship, the cost of uniforms, learning materials, meals, and other school expenses can put education out of reach. Children who have lost a parent or lack stable family support may face additional challenges getting into school and attending regularly.

Our education program supports children who are already enrolled and those who need help accessing school. We work with families, teachers, and community leaders to understand each child’s circumstances and identify where support can make a difference.

Our goal is to help children attend school, feel supported, and have the opportunity to reach their potential.

What Your Support Provides

Practical support for everyday learning.

Support is guided by each child’s needs and the resources available.

School Fees & Education Costs

Help with school-related expenses that can prevent children from enrolling or continuing their education.

Uniforms & School Essentials

Uniforms, books, and learning materials that help children arrive prepared and participate in school life.

Meals & Wellbeing

Support with food and other essential needs where these form part of a child’s sponsorship arrangements.

Encouragement & Follow-Up

Continued contact with families and schools to understand how children are doing and where further support may be needed.

How Sponsorship Works

Local knowledge guides every step.

  1. 1

    Understand Each Child’s Circumstances

    We work with school staff and local authorities to identify children who need assistance. Visits to families and schools help us understand their living conditions, education needs, and existing support.

  2. 2

    Identify the Support Needed

    We consider the costs and practical barriers affecting each child’s education. This helps us identify appropriate support, whether a child is entering school or continuing their studies.

  3. 3

    Connect Support With Education

    Sponsorship helps meet agreed education expenses, such as school fees, uniforms, and other essentials. Our team can explain the current costs and what a particular sponsorship covers before you commit.

  4. 4

    Stay Connected

    We remain in contact with families and schools as circumstances change. Sponsors can contact our team to learn about communication arrangements and the updates available through the program.
