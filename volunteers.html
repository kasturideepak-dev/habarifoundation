Requirements

Local and international volunteers are welcome. You can help in classrooms, health outreach, the school project, coffee and tree planting, or by joining a Kilimanjaro charity climb. We work with children, families and community leaders in Moshi, Kilimanjaro.

  • A willingness to work with children and families with respect
  • Time in Moshi, or a commitment to write and sponsor from home
  • Contact Director Stan Wilfred to arrange your placement
