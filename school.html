School Building Project

Help build a place where children can learn and thrive.

Habari Foundation is seeking support to establish a nursery and primary school for children in Tanzania who face barriers to education.

Our goal is to create a welcoming learning environment where children can build strong foundations, develop their confidence, and prepare for secondary education.

Our Vision for the School

Quality education within reach of more families.

Every child deserves the opportunity to learn, regardless of their family’s financial circumstances.

Our proposed school aims to provide free quality education for children facing significant hardship, including children who have lost a parent and those from low-income households.

The planned model also includes affordable fees for families who can contribute. Together with donor support, this approach is intended to help sustain the school and make education accessible to children with different needs.

We want to build more than classrooms. We want to create a supportive school community where children feel valued and have opportunities to grow.

Why This Project Matters

A suitable learning environment makes a difference.

Access to education depends on more than having a place in a classroom. Children also need learning materials, school furniture, suitable sanitation, and spaces that support teaching and learning.

Habari’s school proposal responds to these needs by bringing essential facilities together in one planned learning environment.

The project aims to:

  • Expand access to nursery and primary education for children facing financial hardship.
  • Provide classrooms equipped for teaching and learning.
  • Support literacy, foundational skills, and preparation for secondary school.
  • Include sanitation and water facilities that support daily school life.
  • Create opportunities to learn through books and technology.
What We Plan to Build

Essential spaces for learning and everyday school life.

The school proposal brings together teaching spaces, supporting facilities, and essential infrastructure. Final scope and delivery will depend on confirmed plans, funding, and local requirements.

Nursery & Primary Classrooms

Dedicated learning spaces where children can develop foundational knowledge and progress through their early education.

Library & Reading Space

A place for children to explore books, strengthen reading skills, and develop curiosity beyond their lessons.

Computer Lab

A planned space for introducing digital skills and supporting learning with technology.

Toilets & Water Facilities

Sanitation facilities and water storage to support pupils and staff throughout the school day.

Dining Hall

A dedicated space to support school meals and daily routines.

School Furniture & Equipment

Desks, seating, learning resources, and equipment needed to make the school functional.

Administration & Storage

Office space for school coordination and secure storage for equipment and supplies.

Supporting Infrastructure

The proposal also includes land, electricity, and school transport as part of the wider project plan.

A Plan for Lasting Support

Build the school. Plan for its future.

Constructing the school is one part of the commitment. Keeping it operating will require continued planning, resources, and community participation.

Our proposed approach combines donor contributions with affordable fees from families who can pay, while aiming to provide free education for children with the greatest financial need.

As the project develops, planning must address teaching, maintenance, learning materials, utilities, and other ongoing needs alongside construction.

We welcome supporters who want to discuss both the school’s development and its longer-term sustainability.

Project Coordination

Local oversight and communication with supporters.

Habari Foundation plans to coordinate the project from its office in the Kilimanjaro region.

The proposed management approach includes an experienced local construction manager to supervise building work, with coordination involving relevant education authorities and other stakeholders.

Project coordinators would report to Habari’s office, with reports compiled for donors and project partners.

Prospective supporters are welcome to contact us about the proposed scope, management arrangements, and information available for review.
