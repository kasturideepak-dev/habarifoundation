Classroom visits with local schools

Volunteers sit beside local teachers in Moshi classrooms — reading, play and extra attention for children who need it. We work with partner nursery and primary schools so every visit supports the lesson already underway.

A happy child is a successful one. We provide a positive, safe and stimulating environment where all are valued.

As we work with school management and local government, we always do a physical assessment of families and schools. Living standards and real need on the ground come first — not a form from far away.

You can join for a single morning or a longer placement. Local and international volunteers are welcome. Director Stan Wilfred arranges each visit with the school so children are not disrupted.

We intend that all children enjoy learning, achieve their potential and become independent life-long learners.

Write to habarifoundation@gmail.com or call +255 754 044 692 to arrange a date. If you are already coming to Moshi for a charity climb, a classroom visit is part of the days before the mountain.
