A Journey With Purpose

Your adventure can reach beyond the mountain.

Habari Foundation is based in Moshi, in Tanzania’s Kilimanjaro region. Our charity climbs bring together people who want to experience the mountain and contribute to children’s education.

Our published charity-climb model allocates 25% of the agreed package amount to students’ needs and 75% toward tour costs. Before booking, ask our team for a written breakdown explaining how this applies to your chosen package, including any separate registration fees.

You can also ask about opportunities to learn more about Habari’s community work while in Moshi. Any school or family visit must be arranged in advance, respect privacy, and fit the needs and schedules of those involved.

Machame Route

A seven-day mountain journey

The Machame itinerary takes you through changing landscapes, from forest trails to open moorland and the upper mountain.

The planned route includes Shira, Lava Tower, Barranco, Karanga, and Barafu before the summit attempt and descent through Mweka.

Itinerary overview

  1. Day 1Machame Gate to Machame Camp.
  2. Day 2Continue to Shira Camp.
  3. Day 3Trek via Lava Tower to Barranco Camp.
  4. Day 4Cross Barranco Wall and continue to Karanga.
  5. Day 5Reach Barafu Camp and prepare for the summit attempt.
  6. Day 6Attempt Uhuru Peak, then descend to the overnight camp.
  7. Day 7Descend to Mweka Gate and transfer from the mountain.

Lemosho Route

A seven-day itinerary approaching from the west

The Lemosho itinerary begins on Kilimanjaro’s western side, travelling through forest and across the Shira area before joining the southern circuit.

The proposed journey includes Lava Tower, Barranco, Karanga, and Barafu before the summit attempt and descent.

Itinerary overview

  1. Day 1Begin the forest trek to Mti Mkubwa Camp.
  2. Day 2Continue through the Shira area to Shira II.
  3. Day 3Trek via Lava Tower to Barranco Camp.
  4. Day 4Cross Barranco Wall and continue to Karanga.
  5. Day 5Continue to Barafu Camp.
  6. Day 6Attempt Uhuru Peak, then descend.
  7. Day 7Complete the descent to Mweka Gate.

Ask our team about available itinerary lengths and the time allowed for acclimatization before choosing your package.

Request the Lemosho Itinerary

Marangu Route

A five-day itinerary with hut accommodation

The Marangu itinerary uses mountain huts for overnight accommodation and follows a route through Mandara, Horombo, and Kibo.

The summit attempt approaches Uhuru Peak via Gilman’s Point, followed by a return descent through Horombo.

Itinerary overview

  1. Day 1Marangu Gate to Mandara Hut.
  2. Day 2Continue to Horombo Hut.
  3. Day 3Trek to Kibo Hut.
  4. Day 4Attempt Uhuru Peak and descend to Horombo Hut.
  5. Day 5Return to Marangu Gate.

Ask about longer itinerary options and acclimatization time when discussing this route.

Before You Book

Get a clear picture of your trip.

Kilimanjaro is a demanding high-altitude trek. Discuss preparation and acclimatization with your operator, and seek appropriate medical advice about your fitness to participate. Reaching the summit cannot be guaranteed.

Request written confirmation of:

  • The full itinerary and total trip duration.
  • The climb operator and arrangements for guides and mountain crew.
  • Accommodation on the mountain and before or after the trek.
  • Meals, drinking water, equipment, and transfers included.
  • Park fees and other charges.
  • The charitable contribution and any registration fee.
  • Costs you must arrange separately, including travel, insurance, equipment, and tips.
  • Mountain safety procedures, emergency arrangements, and insurance requirements.
  • Payment, cancellation, and refund terms.
