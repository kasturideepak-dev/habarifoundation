Get to know Habari Foundation

Helping each other can make the world better

Habari Foundation is a non-governmental, non-political, non-profit and faith-based charity founded in 2018. Based in Moshi, Kilimanjaro, we support education for children from poor families, orphans and street children — working with communities to find long-term solutions to child vulnerability in Tanzania.

  • Our mission

    Support children in education, health and basic needs to help eradicate poverty and ignorance.

  • Our story

    We identify vulnerable families, build trust, and walk with children through school and reintegration.
